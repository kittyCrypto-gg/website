# Atomic Age proof grammar

This proof is intentionally isolated from the real theme. It is a visual checkpoint, not an implementation of the website theme.

## Hard geometry rules

1. Large panels and windows are rectangles transformed by a single coherent shear. Opposite sides remain parallel. No corner is moved independently.
2. Buttons are four-corner controls. The approved form is a rectangle with one slanted side. No extra vertices, blobs, rounded pills, or random cuts.
3. Starbursts are constructed from thin straight rays. Rays have deliberately unequal lengths and uneven angular spacing. A few selected rays may end in small dots. There is no filled central burst polygon.
4. Atoms are constructed from clean ellipses rotated around one centre. Orbit paths remain ellipses. The nucleus is compact and electrons are small circles.
5. Organic Atomic Age motifs may exist later, but they do not replace structural geometry.

## Composition rules

- Asymmetry must be balanced, not random.
- Repetition is deliberate. Repeated controls should share one shape family.
- Negative space is part of the design.
- Motifs are sparse editorial accents, not wallpaper.
- The page must remain readable before decoration is added.

## Colour rules for this proof

- Warm paper is the dominant field.
- Dark ink carries text and outlines.
- Teal is the principal structural ink.
- Coral is the secondary accent.
- Mustard is punctuation only.
- Repeated controls do not become a rainbow.

## Explicit exclusions

This proof must not copy CSS, SVG paths, colour values, class structure, pseudo-element layouts, or component styling from either abandoned Atomic Age implementation. It is built only from the geometry and composition rules agreed in discussion.
